For core, security, police, or administration changes:
1. Perform a read-only investigation first.
2. Map client/server events, Tunnel/Proxy interfaces, permissions, state, and dependencies.
3. Identify compatibility and exploit risks.
4. Present a scoped implementation plan.
5. Do not edit core until the plan is approved.
6. Report what was tested in FiveM and what remains unverified.
